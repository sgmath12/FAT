% NOTES FOR THE NATURAL-TEACHER PARAGRAPH (2026-09-03), from reading the released code.
%
% LBGAT (Cui et al., ICCV'21) is the closest published method to ours by mechanism, and the
% comparison has to be made carefully because its age cuts both ways.  Its loss is
%     MSE(f_s(x_adv), f_t(x)) + CE(f_t(x), y) + beta * KL(f_s(x_adv) || f_s(x)),
% both models in one optimizer.  So: natural network, read at the clean point, matched with a squared
% error and no temperature -- three of the four choices we make.  The fourth is the layer: its target
% is the logit, ours is the feature one layer earlier.  And its natural branch is trained jointly,
% which means the "free teacher" framing does not apply to it -- the second model is paid for during
% training rather than obtained beforehand.
%
% Do NOT bill it as "the closest prior work" in the body until we have run it.  Two reasons.  A 2021
% paper billed as our nearest neighbour invites the reading that the contribution is a five-year-old
% idea with one layer moved, and we would be handing a reviewer that sentence.  And the claim we
% actually want -- feature beats logit at the same read point with no temperature on either side --
% is a measurement we do not yet have.  Run it first; the prominence should follow the number.
%
% HAT (Rade & Moosavi-Dezfooli, ICLR'22) also uses a naturally trained network, but for something
% else entirely, and grouping it with the distillation methods would be wrong:
%     x_hr = x + h (x_adv - x),  h = 2.0
%     y_hr = std_model(x_adv).argmax()          <- label read at x_adv, not at x and not at x_hr
%     loss = CE(f(x), y) + beta KL(f(x_adv) || f(x)) + gamma CE(f(x_hr), y_hr)
% i.e. TRADES plus a helper term.  The natural model supplies LABELS for points beyond the ball, never
% a target for the student to match, so nothing of its representation transfers.  It belongs in the
% trade-off paragraph with ReBAT and RPAT, not in the natural-teacher one.
% Official CIFAR-10 ResNet-18 recipe: beta 2.5, gamma 0.5, helper model = 50 epochs of plain CE.
