the feature anchor, a representation-level bound, and the inherited classifier used in Method.
The table motivates the feature choice; the proposition does not prove that features outperform
logits. The abstract is unchanged. Only the opening paragraph of Method was aligned with Analysis;
the remaining Method claims below still need revision.

The formal setup now specifies vectorized images in `[0,1]^D`, class labels, feature dimension,
Euclidean feature distances, the pixel-clipped perturbation set, and continuous maps with fixed
network statistics. The redundant corollary was absorbed into the interpretation. The new
fixed-head logit bound is an operator-norm consequence, not a robust-accuracy guarantee.

## Feature-variation numbers withheld pending remeasurement

The previous text reported L=7.58, F=6.30, student variation=2.16, and teacher variation=6.68.
Their exact source is `D:/research/FAT/logs/measure_FLO.log`, generated by
`D:/research/FAT/scratchpad/measure_FLO.py` for `l2_bestrecipe_angeps`.

Problems found in the source:

- The values are averages over a training-loader subset, not pointwise quantities.
- The variation is clean-to-adversarial displacement at a student-generated input, not an
  independently optimized pairwise feature diameter. The teacher uses that same input.
- The script uses `train_eps`, which is 8.8/255 in this config, not the stated 8/255.
- The checkpoint is an earlier head-KD configuration, not the current frozen-head CFA model.
- Most importantly, `utils.py::inner_featdir_only_return` switches the student to training mode
  before returning. The measurement script does not restore evaluation mode before extracting
  clean and adversarial student features; the teacher remains in evaluation mode. Consequently,
  the reported distances do not compare the fixed evaluation-mode maps assumed in the proposition.

The numerical stability paragraph was removed from both Analysis and the compiled Appendix text.
The source files and raw results were not changed. They should not be relabeled as validated
stability evidence without remeasurement.

A corrected diagnostic should use an identified current checkpoint and deterministic test split,
explicitly restore evaluation mode after every attack, and keep both networks' statistics fixed.
Report the sample count, radius, feature normalization, attack objective/steps/restarts, and
aggregation. Distinguish sampled anchor distance, exact clean discrepancy, and attack-based
displacement; a sampled displacement lower-bounds diameter but cannot certify its worst case.
Comparisons on shared student-generated attacks and teacher-specific attacks answer different
questions and should be labeled accordingly.

## Remaining Method issues (not rewritten here)

1. A natural teacher's clean geometry can matter for robustness. Its absence at perturbed inputs
   does not imply it cannot contribute to robustness.
2. Temperature one does not remove dark knowledge: a non-one-hot target still contains relative
   probabilities. Conventional shared-temperature KD can also have zero clean discrepancy at
   teacher initialization.
3. A frozen classifier is possible with logit matching too. Feature matching additionally constrains
   representation directions that the head may not observe; inheritance is a design choice, not
   an exclusive mathematical property of feature supervision.
4. Equal mean pixel radius is not equal attack strength. The first-order radius argument also
   needs to distinguish the derived l1 gradient norm from the implemented l2 proxy.
5. Normalized inverse-gradient allocation under a fixed sum cannot give literally unbounded
   individual radii. The issues are concentration and division by zero; the initialization and
   regularization rule need to be stated correctly.

These are substantive claim/implementation issues, not matters that polished prose can resolve.
