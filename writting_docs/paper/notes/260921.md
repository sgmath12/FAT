## 1. "The geometry diagnostics are measured on earlier models, not the final one" (R1, R3, R4)

Recomputed with `scripts/geometry_final.py` on the shipped models: CFA at the final recipe
(`cfa_eps8_150ep`, 150 epochs, $8/255$, WA and AWP, frozen head) against label cross-entropy at the same
stack and warm start (`at_teacherinit_matched`). One protocol for both: 5000 test images, true-label PGD
at $8/255$ with 10 steps, unit-normalized features.

| | clean $S_w/S_b$ | clean own | clean gap | adv. $S_w/S_b$ | adv. own | adv. gap | displacement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CFA (final) | **0.709** | **26.3°** | 29.2° | **1.969** | **33.5°** | 19.2° | 0.312 |
| label CE (final) | 3.744 | 52.2° | **33.5°** | 5.611 | 55.0° | **28.2°** | **0.281** |
| CFA (earlier, appendix) | 0.898 | 28.1° | 28.6° | 2.681 | 33.3° | 16.2° | 0.275 |
| label CE (earlier) | 4.960 | 45.4° | 21.6° | 7.143 | 47.0° | 18.3° | 0.254 |

**Reproduces.** Within-class concentration: the anchored model's clean scatter ratio is $5.3\times$
smaller than label training's ($5.5\times$ before) and $2.85\times$ smaller under attack ($2.7\times$
before). The sample-to-centroid angle gap is larger on the final models, $26.3°$ against $52.2°$.

**Does not reproduce, and the paper has to change.** The between-class claim flips. On the earlier models
the anchor's centroids were farther apart ($28.6°$ against $21.6°$); on the final models label training's
are ($33.5°$ against $29.2°$, and $28.2°$ against $19.2°$ under attack). So the sentence "its clean
clusters are tighter and their centroids are farther apart" is only half true at the configuration we
report: tighter yes, farther apart no. The claim to keep is within-class concentration.

Displacement reproduces in the unhelpful direction as before: the anchored student moves $0.312$ of its
feature norm under attack against label training's $0.281$, so anchoring does not buy smaller
displacement, which the appendix already said.

## 2. "Is the feature metric doing the work?" (R2's framing, R3's novelty question)

Logit MSE with the same fixed head, the same stack and its own sensitivity-matched radius, at the final
recipe:

| 150 epochs, $8/255$, WA + AWP | Clean | AA | NRR |
|---|---|---|---|
| feature MSE, uniform radius | 62.40 | 27.84 | 38.50 |
| feature MSE, sensitivity-matched | **64.75** | **27.98** | **39.07** |
| fixed-head logit MSE, sensitivity-matched | 64.49 | 27.85 | 38.90 |

Feature over logit: $+0.26$ clean, $+0.13$ AA, $+0.17$ NRR, all inside the student-seed spread. The tie
now holds at the reported configuration, not only in the base regime and the 100-epoch stack, which is
what lets the abstract say the mechanism is fixed clean-target regression. A second reading: logit MSE
with the sample-wise radius beats feature MSE with the uniform radius by $2.09$ clean, so the allocation
is not specific to the feature loss either. Its uniform twin was not run, so no radius delta is claimed
for the logit objective.

## 3. "Does the radius gain repeat?" (all four reviewers)

Completed. The final 150-epoch, $8/255$ comparison repeats at student seeds 0, 1, and 2 with the same
teacher and training stack.

| seed | uniform clean / AA | sensitivity clean / AA | $\Delta$ clean | $\Delta$ AA |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| 0 | 62.40 / 27.84 | 64.75 / 27.98 | +2.35 | +0.14 |
| 1 | 62.65 / 28.23 | 64.89 / 28.00 | +2.24 | -0.23 |
| 2 | 62.84 / 27.97 | 64.81 / 28.15 | +1.97 | +0.18 |

Uniform radii give $62.63\pm0.22$ clean and $28.01\pm0.20$ AA; sensitivity matching gives
$64.82\pm0.07$ clean and $28.04\pm0.09$ AA. The paired change is
$+2.19\pm0.20$ clean and $+0.03\pm0.23$ AA. The clean gain therefore repeats in every seed, while
the data support unchanged robust accuracy.
