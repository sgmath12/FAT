\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Experimental Settings}

\paragraph{Datasets.} CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100 \citep{krizhevsky2009learning} and Tiny-ImageNet-200
\citep{le2015tiny}. Random crop with 4-pixel padding and random horizontal flip; no other
augmentation.

\paragraph{Teacher.} For each dataset, a network of the same architecture as the student, trained
naturally for 200 epochs on the same data (\Cref{tab:teachers}). No adversarial training is
performed at any point in the teacher's construction.

\paragraph{Training details.} ResNet-18 throughout the main tables, WideResNet-34-10 in
\Cref{sec:wrn}. The student is initialized from the teacher and trained for 100 epochs with
AdamW at learning rate $0.021$ under a one-cycle schedule, batch size $128$. The attack is 10-step PGD
at step size $2/255$ with a training radius of $8.8/255$; \Cref{sec:radius} varies that radius.
Weight averaging ($\kappa=0.999$, from $20\%$ of the run) and an AWP proxy \citep{2020_awp}
($\gamma=0.005$, after epoch $10$) are used and ablated in \Cref{sec:components}. Every dataset
uses this identical configuration; only the dataset name and the teacher checkpoint change.

\paragraph{Comparison models.} Every baseline in \Cref{tab:main} is run here rather than quoted, so
that the rows are comparable to each other: PGD-AT, TRADES and MART as standard adversarial training;
ARD, RSLAD, AdaAD and IGDM as adversarial distillation, given our natural teacher in place of the
robust one they assume; and RPAT++ reproduced from its own repository. Each row states in its
\emph{Teacher} column what it assumes available. Methods we could not run are left empty with the
reason, and the published figures for all of them---including ADR, CURE, ARREST, LBGAT, Generalist++
and B-MTARD---are collected separately in \Cref{tab:published}.

\paragraph{Evaluation.} Clean accuracy, FGSM, PGD-20/10/50, CW$_\infty$ and AutoAttack (standard
version) on the full test set at $\epsilon=8/255$. NRR is the harmonic mean of clean and AutoAttack
accuracy. All of our numbers are the final-epoch weight-averaged model, with no best-checkpoint
selection.

Two readings, and the first is why these numbers are here rather than in
\Cref{tab:main}. \textbf{Changing the teacher costs these objectives $4.5$ to $9$ points of
AutoAttack while barely moving standard accuracy}---ARD $24.77 \to 20.24$ and $49.49 \to 43.75$,
RSLAD $26.76 \to 21.30$ and $52.13 \to 42.91$, AdaAD $28.06 \to 23.19$ and $52.79 \to 46.22$. What
those methods deliver is therefore mostly attributable to the teacher, which is why their published
numbers cannot be set beside a row produced without one.

Second, \textbf{with the natural teacher every one of them lands below simply not distilling}: on both
datasets, plain adversarial training initialized from the same teacher beats all four. The accuracy a
natural teacher holds is not recovered by treating it as a logit target.

AdaAD\,+\,IGDM collapses to chance on both datasets at the published $\alpha = 20$ ($1.00$ on
CIFAR-100, $10.00$ on CIFAR-10), so ten classes did not rescue it. This is not a porting artifact:
with a natural teacher the IGDM target $\mathrm{softmax}(f_t(x{+}\delta) - f_t(x{-}\delta))$ places
$0.917$ of its mass on a single class and the term reaches $17.3\times$ the AdaAD loss. Reducing the
weight to $\alpha = 1$, which puts the two terms on comparable scales and is the most favourable
reading available, trains normally and still finishes far below AdaAD alone. \Cref{tab:linearity}
gives the structural reason.

Our adversarially trained network reproduces IGDM's reported value, so the measurement is calibrated;
the natural teacher is forty times further from linear, and the finite difference IGDM matches is not
a gradient there.

\paragraph{The anchor against the label, at matched everything.} `at_teacherinit_matched' is plain
PGD-AT started from the same natural teacher, with the same weight averaging, the same AWP, the same
schedule and the same training radius---byte-identical configurations apart from the loss. Against it
the anchor gains \textbf{$+4.92$ clean and $+2.31$ AutoAttack} ($62.65 / 28.77$ against
$57.73 / 26.46$). Both halves of the comparison change together, since we also attack the anchor
rather than the label; \Cref{tab:ablation} separates the two by reverting only the attack.

The sensitivity rule contributes exactly $+0.49$ NRR at both schedule lengths ($+1.61/{+}0.21$ at 50
epochs, $+1.77/{+}0.19$ at 100) at identical total attack budget. AWP changes sign with schedule
length: $-0.25$ NRR at 50 epochs, $+1.21$ at 100. And 50 epochs with weight averaging alone already

the gain is not the stack.

full recipe the same holds---freezing the head scores $62.65/28.77/$NRR $39.43$ against
$62.35/28.68/39.29$ with the head-KD term retained---and this is what removes $\tau$ and $\beta$ from
the method.

\subsection{Training Radius, Teacher Length, and Larger Architectures}
\label{sec:radius}
\label{sec:geometry}
\label{sec:wrn}

\paragraph{The training radius is a plateau, not a tuned point.} Sweeping
$\epsilon_{\mathrm{train}} \in \{8, 8.8, 10\}/255$ with everything else fixed and evaluating at
$8/255$ throughout (\Cref{tab:radius}), NRR peaks at $8.8/255$ on both datasets but the curve is mild,
and the standard $8/255$ already exceeds the best published result on either ($38.72$ against ADR's
$38.08$; $64.48$ against CURE's $63.19$). Past $8.8/255$ the radius buys nothing: AutoAttack and CW at
$10/255$ are identical to the last decimal to those at $8.8/255$ on both datasets, while standard
accuracy falls $2.66$ and $2.29$---the $\epsilon$-independence \Cref{app:robustness} predicts above the
threshold.

\paragraph{What the teacher transfers is geometry, not accuracy.} Changing only the teacher checkpoint
and leaving the student configuration untouched (\Cref{tab:teacherladder}), teacher accuracy and
student accuracy are anti-correlated at $r = -0.999$ across five teachers trained for 50 to 300
epochs: the teacher gains $2.51$ points and the student \emph{loses} $2.04$. NRR peaks in the middle,
so the teacher's training length is a control on the operating point, and locating it costs five
natural-training runs and no adversarial ones. The effect reproduces on Tiny-ImageNet where teacher
accuracy is controlled to $0.32$ points, so accuracy cannot be the cause; what varies monotonically is
the teacher's class separation.

\paragraph{Larger architectures.} WideResNet-34-10 at the same recipe on both CIFAR datasets is
reported in \Cref{tab:w10,tab:w100}, alongside the published WideResNet rows.
