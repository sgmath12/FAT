\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A natural teacher can guide adversarial training through a single clean-feature target, without a
separate label loss. Yet the teacher checkpoint matters: later natural training shifts the student
toward robustness in two trajectories, and similar teacher clean accuracy need not yield similar
student clean accuracy. With the teacher fixed, sensitivity-based radii improve clean accuracy at
nearly unchanged AutoAttack accuracy in the final recipe. CFA thus separates three decisions often
treated together in natural-teacher distillation: the target, the teacher, and the perturbation
budget.
