\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A natural teacher can guide adversarial training through one clean target without a label loss.
Its checkpoint changes the student's clean--robust operating point, while sensitivity-based radii
improve clean accuracy at nearly unchanged AutoAttack accuracy. Clean-logit regression reaches a
similar endpoint, locating the broader mechanism in fixed clean-target regression rather than a
uniquely superior feature metric. CFA thus separates the target, teacher, and perturbation budget.
