\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Clean Feature Anchoring uses a naturally trained model's clean representation as the sole target
during adversarial training. The fixed anchor jointly controls teacher fidelity and local feature
variation, while sensitivity-based radius allocation improves the measured clean--robust trade-off.
Experiments across three datasets show gains over label training that persist under changes to the
training recipe, with further improvements from weight averaging and AWP. Direct logit regression
is also effective, supporting the broader value of regression to a clean teacher target. Finally,
higher teacher clean accuracy need not yield higher student clean accuracy, and changes in teacher
class separation are reflected in the student. These findings motivate selecting natural teachers
for the resulting student trade-off and investigating which aspects of their geometry aid transfer.
