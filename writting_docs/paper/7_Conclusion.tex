\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A natural teacher can guide adversarial training without a separate label loss, but its checkpoints
are not interchangeable: a more accurate teacher can produce a less accurate student while improving
robustness. CFA exposes this trade-off with one clean feature target, and sensitivity-based radii
allocate the perturbation budget according to that target's local response. Across three datasets,
this yields strong clean--robust trade-offs. More broadly, target, teacher, and perturbation budget
are distinct choices in natural-teacher distillation.
