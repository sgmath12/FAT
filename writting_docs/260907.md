## Detail: schedule sensitivity ("did you overtune the epochs and the learning rate?")

**Epochs.** 50 and 100 exist (`ladder_*_fh_50ep`, `ladder_*_fh_100ep`). Missing: 10, 25, 200.
Cost is roughly linear: 0.25 h, 0.6 h, 4.4 h for the anchor at the shipped recipe. The claim to
support is that the method is not a 100-epoch artifact, so the shipped configuration should be run at
each length with nothing else changed, and the baseline it is read against should be run at the same
lengths where one is cheap (label CE at 10 / 25 / 200 is 0.3 / 0.8 / 5 h).

**Learning rate.** Everything uses AdamW at 0.021 under OneCycle, chosen once and never swept.
A five-point sweep around it (0.007, 0.014, 0.021, 0.03, 0.042) at 100 epochs is 11 h and answers the
question directly. If the curve is flat over a factor of two either side, the number is a plateau
rather than a tuned point, which is the same argument tab:radius makes for the training radius.

## To do: teacher quality, not teacher length

`tab:teacherladder` varies the teacher's training length, but that moves its clean accuracy over a
range of only 2.5 points (75.81 to 78.32 on CIFAR-100), so the paper has no evidence about what a
genuinely weaker teacher does. Nothing in the paper answers "what if your teacher were 60% instead of
78%".

Teachers are cheap: a clean epoch is 6.8 s, so a 10-epoch teacher costs about a minute and a
40-epoch one four minutes. The plan is to train clean teachers at 5 / 10 / 20 / 40 epochs, measure
their clean accuracy, and keep the ones that land near 50, 60 and 70 percent; on CIFAR-10 the same
sweep should reach roughly 50 to 90. The students are the expensive half, at 2.2 h each.

Two things to record per rung, since the ladder result was that geometry rather than accuracy is what
transfers: the teacher's clean accuracy and its class separation $S_w/S_b$.

## Not queued, needs a design decision first

- **ARREST.** Not ported. It initializes from a naturally pretrained network and adds a representation
  distance to the label loss, which makes it the closest published method to ours and the most
  conspicuous empty row in tab:main. Half a day of porting.
- **LBGAT.** Trains its natural branch jointly, so "natural-teacher initialization" has to be defined
  first (student only, or both branches). Its CIFAR-10 cell also diverges at the authors' lr, for the
  reason recorded in `config/CIFAR10/lbgat0_100ep.yaml`.
- **HAT.** Uses the natural network only to label helper points, so an initialization from it is a
  different intervention than for the distillation methods.
- **ADR.** Self-distillation from an EMA of the student; there is no external teacher to swap. Being
  run on other hardware.
- **RPAT++.** Reproduced from the authors' repository rather than in this harness, so a natural-init
  variant needs a port first.
- **TRADES / MART at their own recipe with a natural init.** Cheap (about 1.5 h each) but the standard
  baselines were assigned to another machine, so they are listed rather than queued.
